The producer

Jonathan Desir

Executive Producer

Jonathan's older brother Julio, an agency creative director, got him his first internship in high school. He started at Red Car as an intern and left as its executive producer. Next came almost ten years as an EP at Optimus, where he produced post on Magnificent Coloring World and Shaq's Icy Hot campaign, then four years running the post team at WoW Studios, Whirlpool's in-house content studio.

He's worked with directors from John Singleton to Joe Pytka, and mentoring the next generation of editors and producers has always been part of how he works.

Available now for executive producer and head-of-post roles, full-time or contract.

The voice
