The producer

Jonathan Desir

Executive Producer

Jonathan has spent more than twenty years running production and post. He started as an intern at Red Car and worked his way up to executive producer, then spent nine years as an EP at Optimus, where he produced post on Chance the Rapper's Magnificent Coloring World and Shaquille O'Neal's Icy Hot campaign. Most recently he was executive producer at WoW Studios, Whirlpool's in-house content studio, handling budgets, bidding, talent schedules, and delivery for a team of editors, animators, and producers.

He's produced for Whirlpool, McDonald's, State Farm, Miller Lite, and the Chicago Bulls, alongside collaborators from Chance the Rapper to John Singleton and Joe Pytka. Mentoring the next generation of creative talent is a big part of how he works.

Open to executive producer and head-of-post roles, full-time or contract.
